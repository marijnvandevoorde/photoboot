Photoboot

A photo booth that prints stickers

Turn a laptop, tablet or Mac mini with a camera into a party photo booth. It prints black-and-white stickers on a cheap Bluetooth thermal printer, and guests get a colour copy on their phone by QR code.

Open the booth

What you need

  • A device with a camera: a laptop, a tablet or a Mac mini with a webcam. Open boot.small-victories.co in Chrome or Edge (Android, Mac, Windows, ChromeOS), or in the Bluefy browser on iPad and iPhone. Nothing to install and no account.
  • A Bluetooth thermal printer: Phomemo, the "cat printers" from AliExpress and Temu, LuckJingle, the Action and Lidl pocket printers, PeriPage, Paperang, Niimbot or a Bluetooth receipt printer. It's built and tested on a Phomemo P2S.
  • No printer yet? "Start without printer" works on anything with a camera; guests get their photo by QR code.

What guests get

  • A live camera with a big countdown, a flash and a cancel button. Tap anywhere to start.
  • Single photos or strips of 2 to 4 shots.
  • Looks shown live on the camera before the photo is taken: Classic, Pop art, Woodcut and Stipple, plus the Mirror and Big head twists.
  • A preview of the sticker exactly as it prints, then Print, Get photo (a QR code), Retake or Done.
  • English, Dutch or French, or your own wording.

What the host gets

  • Your own sticker design: a typed title, names, date or hashtag, or your own header and footer artwork.
  • It runs unattended: auto-print, copies and a print limit per photo, an idle screen, a paper-low warning for the host, and camera and printer connections that recover by themselves.
  • Guest-proof: once started it stays on the camera, and leaving it takes your PIN.
  • A colour copy of every photo on the booth device, to download as a ZIP the next morning.
  • An optional event gallery (paid, once per event): every photo from all your booths in one private online gallery, with a download of all of them, kept for a year.

Photos and privacy

  • Without a gallery, a photo only leaves the booth when a guest asks for it by QR code. It gets a private link, the guest can delete it, and it's deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics cookies. Details are in the privacy policy.

Open source

Photoboot is open source under the MIT licence; the source code can be self-hosted with Docker. This site is run by Small Victories BV in Belgium. Questions: hello@small-victories.co.
