    Paper

    Phomemo-class printers run at ~11.8 dots/mm. 552 dots = classic 53/50 mm sticker roll. Narrower rolls: 384 dots (32 mm), 288 dots (25 mm). Confirm the real width with calibration before you commit.

    The booth counts the paper each sticker uses and shows “📄 low” on its printer badge — guests don't see a warning. Tap New paper roll above after changing the roll.
