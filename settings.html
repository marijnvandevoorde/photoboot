    Printer & paper

    Paper

    Pick the roll in the printer. The standard 50 mm sticker roll is the first one.

    The printer makes about 12 dots per mm: 552 = the standard 50 mm sticker, 384 = 32 mm, 288 = 25 mm. Test it with a calibration print first.

    Dark gives the smoothest photos (dots merge instead of speckling). Try Normal if faces turn into black blobs.

    The booth counts the paper each sticker uses and shows “📄 low” on the printer badge, so you know when to change rolls. Guests don't see a warning.

    Printing

    The sticker prints as soon as the photo is taken. Guests can't pick a look first.

    Handy for couples: 2 gives everyone in the photo their own sticker.

      Security

      Host PIN

      Asked whenever someone tries to leave the booth: these settings, changing the printer or stopping the booth. It stops curious guests; it isn't a bank vault.

      Booth lock

      Once you start the booth it stays on the camera, even if someone reloads the page or the app restarts, and it reconnects to the same printer by itself. Stop booth unlocks it: the next start shows the “Connect printer” screen again. You can also stop it from the booth: long-press the top-left corner of the camera picture for 2 seconds.

      Lock the whole tablet too

      The booth can't stop guests from leaving the app with the home button or a swipe. The tablet itself can:

      • iPad / iPhone: turn on Guided Access in Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access and set a passcode. Open the booth, then triple-click the side (or home) button to start it. Triple-click again and enter the passcode to stop.
      • Android: turn on App pinning (Settings → Security → App pinning, the name varies by brand), open the booth, then pin it from the recent-apps screen. On a dedicated device, Chrome's kiosk mode does the same.
